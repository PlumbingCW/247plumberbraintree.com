What to have ready when you call

A few details help us size up the job in the first minute.

  • Your street address and town.
  • What you see, hear or smell.
  • Whether water is still running and the main valve is off.
  • The age and type of the fixture or equipment if you know it.
  • A good callback number in case the call drops.

Hours and what counts as an emergency

We answer 24 hours a day, seven days a week. An emergency is anything that is damaging your home or putting people at risk, such as a burst pipe, sewage backing up or flooding from a failed pump.

Less urgent problems like a dripping faucet or a slow drain are scheduled for a same-day appointment when one is available. If you are unsure which category you are in, call and ask.

If you smell gas

Leave the building right away, do not touch switches or phones inside, and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Call us once you are safe.
