Braintree Highlands, Braintree, MA

Braintree Highlands is the residential section of Braintree that runs along the southern stretch of Washington Street, away from the older village centers. If you live here and need a plumber in Braintree Highlands, MA, call (617) 752-0573, describe what you see, and ask when someone can come out.

The Massachusetts Historical Commission's 1979 town survey says the Highlands was laid out as a street grid in the 1870s but mostly built up in the twentieth century. A real estate guide to the area puts the median build year near 1954, with ranches, split-levels, Capes and some newer custom homes. Those are secondary figures, so treat them as a general picture.

What it means for you: many Highlands houses are old enough that fixtures and water heaters have been replaced at least once. The age of each part matters more than the age of the house.

Plumbing calls that fit a Highlands house

Detached single-family homes on ordinary residential lots tend to generate the same handful of calls. Here is how we sort them.

A tank that drips at the base or gives lukewarm water is usually near the end. See water heater replacement for how repair and replacement are weighed.

Who owns which pipe on your property

Town of Braintree rules give the homeowner responsibility for the sewer lateral from the building to the property line on a public street, and for the water service pipe from the curb stop into the house. The Town handles the section in the street. The sewer rules and the matching clause are not worded identically, so confirm the dividing point with the Braintree Water and Sewer Department before assuming who pays for a repair.

This matters in the Highlands because most houses sit on their own lots with their own service lines. A pressure drop or a slow main drain may be a private-side problem that is yours to fix, or a street-side problem that is the Town's. A plumber who starts at the curb stop and the cleanout can tell you which side of the line you are on.

Check your sump discharge before the next storm

Braintree's sewer regulations prohibit sending sump pump, roof leader and foundation drain water into the sanitary sewer. If your pump line ties into a sewer pipe, the Town can require it to be corrected. A plumber can trace where yours goes before an inspector or a storm finds out for you.

Lead and galvanized service lines in Braintree

The Town of Braintree has an inventory under way to identify lead and galvanized water service lines. Affected owners were told to expect a letter, and anyone who did not receive one should assume their service is neither.

If you are replacing a water heater or repairing the line anyway, ask the plumber what material your service pipe is. They can see it where it enters the house, and that answer helps you decide how much to do in one visit.
