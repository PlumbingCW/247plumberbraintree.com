Braintree 24/7 Plumbing operates from 875 Washington Street in Braintree, Massachusetts. Our job is to answer the phone at every hour, understand what is wrong and get the right licensed plumber to your door.

Plumbing problems rarely wait for business hours. A split supply line, a backed up main or a dead water heater in January all land at the worst times, so the line stays open all night.

Every call is handled the same way, whether it comes at noon or at three in the morning. We ask direct questions, give you safe next steps and explain the price before anyone starts work.

What we do and what we do not do

We handle emergency repairs, drain and sewer work, water heaters, leak detection and installations for homes and small businesses. The full list is on the services page, and each service page explains how the problem is found.

We do not offer anything through forms or messages. Every request is a phone call, so you speak with a person and get answers while the problem is still fresh.

Why a local base matters

Braintree has its own Water and Sewer Department, its own plumbing inspector and housing that skews old, with a median build year of 1957 according to the Census Bureau. Knowing which parts of a pipe belong to the owner and which belong to the Town saves time and surprises.

We also cover Quincy, Weymouth, Randolph and Holbrook, the towns that border Braintree. Each has its own water supplier and permit office, which is why they have separate pages in our service areas.

How to check any plumber, including us

Massachusetts regulates plumbing and gas fitting through the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters, which issues journeyman and master licenses. The state publishes an occupational license lookup, and a homeowner can use it to confirm that a plumber's license is current before work starts.

Permits matter too. Under the state plumbing rules, work such as replacing a water heater needs a permit before it is installed, and the permit is issued to a properly licensed person. Ask any plumber who will pull the permit and who will be at the inspection. That one question tells you a lot about how the job will go.

We are glad to answer those questions on the phone. If we cannot answer one, we will say so and point you to the Town's Inspector of Plumbing and Gas at Town Hall.
